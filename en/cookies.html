Cookie Policy

Last updated: 5 October 2026

1. What we use

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies and does not track your browsing, so we do not ask for consent through a banner.

2. Third-party content

Some pages load content from external services, which may set cookies or collect technical data (such as your IP address) under their own policies:

  • Web font service — provides the typeface used on the site.

3. How to manage them

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. The website will keep working, but the map on the Contacts page may not be displayed.
