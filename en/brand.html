Our Identity and Manifesto

We raise the standard of the professional market with uncompromising food curation, built on Dalifal's 40 years of experience.

From Controlled Origin to Excellence on the Plate

Neutura was born from the need for a Portuguese product line that meets the market's logistics and volume demands while standing out for its technical consistency and pure flavour. We are not mere distributors: we are rigorous curators of everything that goes into your kitchen.

We work directly with selected producers at source, giving the professional channel clean raw materials with exact calibration, in balance with nature at every stage.

Built for the Professional Channel

With specialised ranges of charcuterie, fish and seafood, ready meals, pulses and infusions, Neutura is designed for high performance and convenience. We focus on technical precision, food safety and zero waste to make professional kitchens more profitable across Horeca, Food Service and Modern Retail.

Discover B2B Solutions

The Answer Lies in Nature

We believe the best flavour comes from respecting land and sea. The name Neutura is no accident: it reflects our commitment to working in harmony with the environment.

From choosing producers with sustainable practices to optimising delivery routes to cut our carbon footprint, every batch we ship carries a promise of environmental responsibility.

Mission, Vision and Values

Our Mission

To provide premium food solutions with consistent industrial calibration, ensuring maximum operational profitability, building strong partnerships and delivering outstanding food value at competitive prices.

Our Vision

To establish Neutura as the trusted seal and first choice in Portugal for chefs, hotels and retailers who accept no compromise on quality and consistency.

Our Values

Uncompromising curation, preventive laboratory control, optimised formats and full transparency across the supply chain, always with respect for nature.

Globally Validated Safety

We operate under systematic audits and microbiological controls to ensure full compliance in every batch we ship.
